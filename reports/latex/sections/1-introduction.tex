\subsection{Goal of the project}
\label{sec:goal}
\guidance{
  Briefly describe what the project is about and which problem it solves.
  \textbf{Must include the success criteria of the project} (course template).
  Think of the ML component as deployed and used in operation, since the course focuses on building and deploying one ML component rather than a whole ML-based system.
}

Someone who owns a used car, or wants to buy one, has to compare listings by hand to find out what it is worth.
We build and deploy an ML component that does this for them: from a description of a used car it estimates the asking price the car would have on a European online car portal.
In the first use case, car valuation (UC1), an owner describes their car and receives a price estimate in euros, together with the properties that influenced it most (FR-06, FR-08).
In the second, purchase guidance (UC2), a buyer describes the car they are looking for and receives a typical price with a 90\,\% interval and comparable real listings (FR-02, FR-07, FR-09).

The learning task is a regression on $\log(\mathrm{price})$.
We train on the AutoScout24 Car Listings Dataset (EDN-01), a snapshot of 118,382 listings with 75 columns from eight European countries.
It is published on Zenodo under DOI \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17643343}{10.5281/zenodo.17643343}.
The scope is used passenger cars (EDN-04) of the 11 makes with at least 300 listings each, which together cover 98.6\,\% of the used listings (EDN-05).
For a car outside this scope the component will return a refusal and no estimate (FR-04).
We hold out all Spanish listings as a market the model has not seen, so that Milestone~6 can show drift detection and retraining on real data (EDN-03).

In operation the component will be a containerised API on the virtual machine the course provides, with 4\,GB of memory, 20\,GB of disk, and no GPU (EDN-17).
CI will build the released model into the image (EDN-08), and a person will decide when to retrain and which model to promote (EDN-12).
The 16 functional and 15 non-functional requirements are described in \cref{sec:m1-requirements}.

\paragraph{Success criteria.}
A model may be deployed only if it meets all six criteria of \cref{tab:success-criteria} on the test split (EDN-06, EDN-15).
The primary metric is the median absolute percentage error (MdAPE).
Together the criteria form the release gate (NFR-01).
The \texttt{evaluate} stage of the pipeline reads the bounds from \texttt{params.yaml}, measures each criterion, and writes the verdict to \texttt{metrics.json} (EDN-58).
SC-04 is also our fairness check.
It compares the error across segments, where a segment is one value of make, country, seller type, fuel category, or age band, such as BMW or cars older than 20 years.

Our baseline B0 predicts the median price of cars with the same make, model, and two-year age band.
We set the bounds after one exploratory run on the final scope, in which B0 reached an MdAPE of 11.9\,\% and a first LightGBM model reached 6.7\,\% (EDN-06).
In the same run, the LightGBM model's MdAPE for cars older than 20 years was 15.3\,\%, just above SC-04's bound.
We kept the bound at 15\,\% and recorded this segment as a known risk.

\paragraph{Result.}
As of the first delivery, no model passes the gate yet, so none is released.
The candidate \texttt{lgbm-basic}, a LightGBM model on the 16 basic features of a car, meets SC-01, SC-02, SC-03, and SC-06.
It misses SC-04 in one of the 26 segments the criterion checks (\cref{fig:segments}): its MdAPE is 16.80\,\% on the 784 test cars older than 20 years, while the next-worst segment is at 10.4\,\%.
SC-05 cannot be measured yet, because the price intervals of UC2 are not implemented: FR-07 is due in Milestone~4, and the calibration split of \cref{tab:split} is held for them.
We accept the miss as a limitation of this model and keep the bound at 15\,\% (EDN-62).

The verdicts on SC-01, SC-02, and SC-04 do not depend on the seed of the seller-grouped split.
We refitted the candidate on 20 other split seeds: the test MdAPE was 6.72\,\% on average with a standard deviation of 0.26 percentage points, against 6.73\,\% on the committed split.
SC-01 and SC-02 held on all 20 splits, and SC-04 was missed on all 20.
Cars older than 20 years averaged 18.2\,\%, so the committed 16.80\,\% is the lowest of the 21 values (EDN-32).

We kept the model simple and put the effort into the engineering practices described in \cref{sec:methodology}.
The decisions behind them, and how AI was involved in each, are recorded in our Engineering Decision Notebook (MLOps\_\TeamName\_EDN.pdf), which this report cites by entry ID.
