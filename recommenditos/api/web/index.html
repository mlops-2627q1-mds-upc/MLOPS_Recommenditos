Describe the car and press Estimate price. You get four things, in this order:

  1. Estimatethe asking price the model expects for this car
  2. Rangewhere 9 in 10 asking prices for cars like it fall
  3. Whythe details that moved the estimate up or down
  4. Listingsreal cars of the same make and model in the data
